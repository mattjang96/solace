# 🩺 Solace — Your Personal Medical Advocates

Solace is a clean and modern web application designed to help users **find trusted medical professionals** with ease.  
Whether you're looking for a doctor, therapist, or health coach — Solace connects you with experts who truly care.


---

## ✨ Features

- **Smart Search:** Instantly search by name, city, specialty, degree, or experience.  
- **Professional Profiles:** Browse detailed profiles with specialties, credentials, and experience levels.  
- **One-Click Contact:** Call or reach out directly through integrated “Call Now” actions.  
- **Location-based Results:** Find professionals near you in cities like New York, Los Angeles, and beyond.  
- **Wide Range of Specialties:** From pediatrics to life coaching — get matched to exactly who you need.
